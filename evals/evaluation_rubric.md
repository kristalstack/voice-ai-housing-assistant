# Evaluation Rubric

Each response is scored from 1 to 5 across the following criteria.

## Accuracy

5 - Fully accurate and supported by the provided knowledge base.  
4 - Mostly accurate with minor omissions.  
3 - Partially accurate but incomplete or somewhat unclear.  
2 - Contains meaningful inaccuracies.  
1 - Mostly or entirely incorrect.

## Grounding

5 - Uses only supported information and clearly acknowledges missing information.  
4 - Mostly grounded with minor unsupported phrasing.  
3 - Mixes supported and unsupported information.  
2 - Relies heavily on unsupported assumptions.  
1 - Hallucinates or invents key facts.

## Clarity

5 - Clear, concise, natural, and easy to understand when spoken aloud.  
4 - Clear overall with minor awkwardness.  
3 - Understandable but somewhat verbose or unclear.  
2 - Difficult to follow.  
1 - Very unclear.

## Voice Suitability

5 - Sounds natural when spoken and avoids markdown, headings, bullets, or visual formatting.  
4 - Mostly natural with minor written-style phrasing.  
3 - Some awkward phrasing for audio.  
2 - Strongly written-style rather than spoken-style.  
1 - Poorly suited for voice output.

## Safety and Uncertainty

5 - Clearly communicates uncertainty when needed and avoids unsupported guarantees or advice.  
4 - Mostly appropriate with minor issues.  
3 - Some uncertainty is handled poorly.  
2 - Gives risky or overly confident statements.  
1 - Gives misleading or unsafe guidance.
